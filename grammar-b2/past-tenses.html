Past Tenses

Past Simple, Continuous, Perfect and Perfect Continuous — all forms, timelines, and common mistakes explained.

Past
Past Simple
Минулий простий час — completed past events with a clear time
Forms
+
regular: worked / played / stopped | irregular: went / saw / made / came
–
Subject + did not (didn't) + V (base form)
?
Did + subject + V?
✎
Spelling: stop→stopped, travel→travelled, study→studied
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Completed action at a specific past time
Завершена дія у конкретний момент минулого
02
Sequence of completed past events
Послідовність завершених минулих подій
03
Past habits or states (no longer true)
Минулі звички або стани (більше не актуальні)
Signal words
yesterdaylast weekagoin 2015when I was youngoncethenat that time
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
She visited Lviv last summer.Минулого літа вона відвідала Львів.specific time
He woke up, made coffee and left.Він прокинувся, зварив каву і пішов.sequence
I didn't understand the question.Я не зрозумів запитання.negation
Past
Past Continuous
Минулий тривалий час — actions in progress at a past moment
Forms
+
I/he/she/it was + V-ing | you/we/they were + V-ing
–
I/he/she/it wasn't + V-ing | you/we/they weren't + V-ing
?
Was he + V-ing? | Were they + V-ing?
Timeline diagram — interrupted action
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Action in progress at a specific past moment
Дія тривала в конкретний момент минулого
02
Longer action interrupted by shorter Past Simple
Тривала дія, перервана короткою (Past Simple)
03
Two parallel past actions (while)
Дві паралельні дії в минулому (while)
04
Background scene-setting in a story
Опис фону / обстановки в розповіді
ℹ
when vs while pattern: when introduces the short interrupting action (Past Simple): "I was cooking when the phone rang." — while introduces the ongoing background action: "While I was cooking, the phone rang."
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
I was reading when he called.Я читав, коли він зателефонував.interrupted action
While I was studying, she was watching TV.Поки я навчався, вона дивилася телевізор.parallel actions
It was raining and the wind was blowing.Йшов дощ і дув вітер.scene-setting
Past
Past Perfect
Минулий перфектний час — the "earlier past" before another past moment
Forms
+
Subject + had (I'd/she'd) + V3
–
Subject + had not (hadn't) + V3
?
Had + subject + V3?
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Action completed BEFORE another past action
Дія, завершена ДО іншої минулої дії
02
With: before / after / when / by the time
З: before / after / when / by the time
03
Reported speech (backshift from Present Perfect)
Непряма мова (зсув з Present Perfect)
04
Third conditional sentences
Третій тип умовних речень
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
When I arrived, she had already left.Коли я прийшов, вона вже пішла.earlier past
He said he had never visited Kyiv.Він сказав, що ніколи не відвідував Київ.reported speech
If I had known, I would have told you.Якби я знав, я б сказав тобі.3rd conditional
✓
When Past Perfect is optional: If the sequence is clear from "before" / "after", Past Simple can be used: "After he left, I called her." = "After he had left, I called her." — Both are correct.
Past
Past Perfect Continuous
Минулий перфектно-тривалий — duration before a past moment
Forms
+
Subject + had been + V-ing
–
Subject + hadn't been + V-ing
?
Had + subject + been + V-ing?
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Ongoing action over a period ending at a specific past moment
Тривала дія до певного моменту в минулому
02
Past cause explaining a past result / state
Минула причина, що пояснює минулий стан
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
She had been waiting for 2 hours when he arrived.Вона чекала 2 години до його приходу.duration before past
He had been running — that's why he was breathless.Він бігав — тому й задихався.past cause
