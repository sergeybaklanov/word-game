Present Tenses

Present Simple, Continuous, Perfect and Perfect Continuous — forms, rules, signal words and examples with Ukrainian notes.

Present
Present Simple
Теперішній простий час — facts, habits, truths, schedules
Forms
+
I/you/we/they work | he/she/it works
–
I/you/we/they don't work | he/she/it doesn't work
?
Do you/they work? | Does he/she/it work?
✎
Spelling: add -s/-es (go→goes, watch→watches) | consonant+y → -ies (study→studies)
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Permanent facts & general truths
Постійні факти та загальні істини
02
Habits, routines, repeated actions
Звички, рутина, повторювані дії
03
Fixed schedules (timetables)
Розклади транспорту, уроків
04
Time clauses with future meaning (when, as soon as, before, after, until)
Підрядні часу з майбутнім значенням
Signal words
alwaysusuallyoftensometimesrarelyneverevery dayon Mondaysgenerally
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule used
Water boils at 100°C.Вода кипить при 100°C.general truth
She goes to the gym every Monday.Вона ходить до спортзалу щопонеділка.habit
The flight leaves at 07:30.Рейс відправляється о 7:30.schedule
I'll call when I arrive.Я подзвоню, коли приїду.time clause + future
⚠
Common mistake Do NOT use Present Simple for actions happening right now. ✗ "She works in the garden now." ✓ "She is working in the garden now."
Present
Present Continuous
Теперішній тривалий час — ongoing, temporary, planned future
Forms
+
I am working | he/she/it is working | we/you/they are working
–
I am not (I'm not) working | he isn't working | they aren't working
?
Am I working? | Is he working? | Are they working?
✎
Spelling: double final consonant (run→running) | drop silent -e (make→making)
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Actions happening at this exact moment
Дії, що відбуваються прямо зараз
02
Temporary situations (not permanent)
Тимчасові ситуації (не постійні)
03
Fixed future arrangements / plans
Заплановані майбутні події
04
Annoying habits with "always" (irritated tone)
Дратівливі звички з "always" (критичний тон)
Signal words
nowright nowat the momentcurrentlytodaythis weeklook!still
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule used
She is reading a report right now.Вона зараз читає звіт.happening now
He is staying with parents this month.Цього місяця він живе з батьками.temporary
We are flying to Warsaw on Friday.У п'ятницю летимо до Варшави.future arrangement
You are always losing your keys!Ти постійно втрачаєш ключі!annoying habit
✗
Stative verbs cannot be continuous ✗ "I am knowing the answer." ✓ "I know the answer." — See the Stative Verbs section.
Present
Present Perfect
Теперішній перфектний час — past action connected to the present
Forms
+
I/you/we/they have (I've) + V3 | he/she/it has (he's) + V3
–
I/you/we/they haven't + V3 | he/she/it hasn't + V3
?
Have you + V3? | Has she + V3?
✎
V3 = past participle: go→gone, see→seen, make→made, work→worked
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use — 4 core uses
01
Past action with a result NOW
Минула дія, результат якої актуальний ЗАРАЗ
02
Life experience — ever / never
Досвід протягом усього життя
03
Unfinished time period — for / since
Незавершений часовий проміжок
04
Very recent — just / already / yet
Нещодавня подія (just/already/yet)
for vs since
for — duration (тривалість)
for 3 years / for two months
for a long time / for ages
since — starting point (початок)
since 2019 / since Monday
since I was a child / since we met
Signal words
justalreadyyeteverneverrecentlylatelysince 2020for 3 yearsso farthis week/year
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
I have lost my keys.Я загубив ключі. (немає зараз)result now
Have you ever tried sushi?Ти коли-небудь куштував суші?life experience
She has worked here for five years.Вона тут працює п'ять років (і досі).unfinished period
He has just called.Він щойно зателефонував.very recent
I haven't finished yet.Я ще не закінчив.yet (negative)
⚠
Present Perfect vs Past Simple Use PP when NO specific time is given. Use Past Simple when a specific time IS given. ✓ "I have seen that film." (no time) vs ✓ "I saw it last Tuesday." (specific time)
Present
Present Perfect Continuous
Теперішній перфектно-тривалий — duration and ongoing activity
Forms
+
I/you/we/they have been + V-ing | he/she/it has been + V-ing
–
I haven't been + V-ing | she hasn't been + V-ing
?
Have you been + V-ing? | Has she been + V-ing?
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Action started in past, still happening NOW (focus on duration)
Дія триває з минулого (акцент на тривалості)
02
Recent activity that explains a present result
Нещодавня дія, що пояснює теперішній стан
03
"How long…?" for ongoing actions
Запитання "як довго…?" для тривалих дій
PP Simple vs PP Continuous
PP Simple — completed / result
I have written three emails. (done, focus on number)
She has cleaned the flat. (it's clean now)
PP Continuous — duration / activity
I have been writing emails all morning. (focus on time spent)
She has been cleaning all day. (she's tired now)
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
I have been waiting for an hour.Я чекаю вже годину.ongoing duration
She has been crying — eyes are red.Вона плакала — очі червоні.visible result
How long have you been learning Ukrainian?Як довго ти вивчаєш українську?how long question
