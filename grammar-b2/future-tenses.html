Future Tenses

will, going to, Future Continuous and Future Perfect — how to choose the right future form and when.

Future
Future Simple — will
Майбутній простий час — predictions, decisions, promises, offers
Forms
+
Subject + will ('ll) + V (same for all persons)
–
Subject + will not (won't) + V
?
Will + subject + V?
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
When to use
01
Spontaneous decisions (made at the moment of speaking)
Спонтанні рішення, прийняті в момент мовлення
02
Predictions based on opinion / belief
Прогнози на основі думки/переконання
03
Promises, offers, requests, threats
Обіцянки, пропозиції, прохання, погрози
04
Future facts (certain / inevitable)
Майбутні факти (певні, неминучі)
will vs going to — key contrast
will — spontaneous / uncertain
"It's hot." → "I will open the window." (spontaneous)
"I think it will rain tomorrow." (opinion)
going to — planned / evidence
"I am going to visit her next week." (planned)
"Look — it is going to rain." (evidence: dark clouds)
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
"The phone is ringing." — "I 'll get it!""Телефон дзвонить." — "Я відповім!"spontaneous
I think she will pass the exam.Я думаю, вона складе іспит.opinion-based
I won't tell anyone, I promise.Я нікому не скажу, обіцяю.promise
Future
Future — be going to
Майбутній час — pre-planned intentions and evidence-based predictions
Forms
+
I am going to go | he/she/it is going to go | we/they are going to go
–
I am not going to go | he isn't going to go | they aren't going to go
?
Are you going to go? | Is she going to go?
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
I am going to study medicine.Я збираюся вивчати медицину.pre-planned intention
Look at those clouds — it is going to rain.Дивись на хмари — зараз піде дощ.evidence-based
Future
Future Continuous
Майбутній тривалий час — action in progress at a future moment
Forms
+
Subject + will be + V-ing
–
Subject + won't be + V-ing
?
Will + subject + be + V-ing?
Timeline diagram
Timeline · Діаграма часу
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
At 9 am tomorrow I will be sitting in my exam.Завтра о 9 ранку я сидітиму на іспиті.future moment
Will you be using the car this evening?Ти будеш користуватися машиною ввечері?polite question
Future
Future Perfect
Майбутній перфектний час — completed before a future deadline
Forms
+
Subject + will have + V3
–
Subject + won't have + V3
?
Will + subject + have + V3?

Timeline · Діаграма часу
Examples
EnglishУкраїнськаRule
