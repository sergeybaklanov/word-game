B2 Topic

Giving & Reacting to Advice

Functional language for advice, from gentle suggestions to firm warnings — and how to accept, doubt, or ask for more detail.

B2 Special Topic
Giving advice
Давати пораду — від найделікатнішої пропозиції до рішучого застереження

English has a whole scale of phrases for giving advice, from a soft hint to a direct warning. Choosing the right level depends on how serious the situation is and how close you are to the listener — a gentle suggestion to a stranger would sound odd as a blunt warning, and vice versa.

1 · Gentle suggestion — a soft, indirect idea
Phrase patternExampleПереклад
Have you thought about + V-ing/noun…?Have you thought about talking to someone?Ти не думав про те, щоб з кимось поговорити?
It might be worth + V-ingIt might be worth trying a different approach.Можливо, варто спробувати інший підхід.
You might want to try + V-ing/noun…You might want to try meditation.Можливо, тобі варто спробувати медитацію.
2 · Personal advice — based on what you would do
Phrase patternExampleПереклад
Personally, I'd + V1Personally, I'd wait before signing anything.Особисто я б почекав, перш ніж щось підписувати.
If I were you, I'd + V1If I were you, I'd ask for a refund.На твоєму місці я б попросив повернути гроші.
✎
Note "If I were you" uses the same unreal were as second conditional — see Conditionals.
3 · Stronger advice — closer to an instruction
Phrase patternExampleПереклад
It's high time you + Past SimpleIt's high time you started looking after your health.Тобі вже давно пора подбати про здоров'я.
You'd be better off + V-ingYou'd be better off saving that money.Тобі було б краще заощадити ці гроші.
You really ought to + V1You really ought to apologise to her.Тобі справді варто перед нею вибачитись.
✎
Note "It's high time + Past Simple" doesn't refer to the past — see Wishes & Regrets for the same structure with wish.
4 · Warning — the strongest level, a clear "don't"
Phrase patternExampleПереклад
Whatever you do, don't + V1Whatever you do, don't tell him about the surprise!Що б не сталося, не кажи йому про сюрприз!
I'd steer clear of + noun/V-ingI'd steer clear of that neighbourhood at night.Я б уникав цього району вночі.
The last thing you want to do is + V1The last thing you want to do is ignore the warning signs.Останнє, що тобі варто робити — ігнорувати тривожні сигнали.
You'll really regret it if you don't + V1You'll really regret it if you don't back up your files.Ти дуже пошкодуєш, якщо не зробиш резервну копію файлів.
B2 Special Topic
Reacting to advice
Реагувати на пораду — погодитись, засумніватися, ввічливо відмовити або попросити уточнення
1 · Accepting — the advice makes sense to you
PhraseExampleПереклад
That makes sense.A: You should ask for a pay rise. B: That makes sense.У цьому є сенс.
I hadn't thought of that.I hadn't thought of that — thanks for the idea!Я про це не подумав.
That's a great idea.That's a great idea, I'll try it tonight.Це чудова ідея.
2 · Doubting or politely rejecting — the advice doesn't quite fit
PhraseExampleПереклад
I've already tried that and…I've already tried that and it didn't work.Я вже пробував так робити, і…
I see what you mean, but…I see what you mean, but I still think it's risky.Я розумію, про що ти, але…
That's easier said than done.That's easier said than done — it's not that simple.Легко сказати, важко зробити.
I'm not sure that would work for me, because…I'm not sure that would work for me, because I don't have a car.Не впевнений, що мені це підійде, бо…
3 · Asking for more — you need a more specific suggestion
PhraseExampleПереклад
Could you be a bit more specific?Could you be a bit more specific about what you mean?Можеш уточнити конкретніше?
What would you do in my position [about …]?What would you do in my position?Що б ти зробив на моєму місці?
✓
Choosing the right level Giving advice moves from indirect to direct: gentle suggestion → personal advice → stronger advice → warning. Reacting moves the other way: accept → doubt/politely reject → ask for a more specific suggestion. Matching the level to the situation (and to how well you know the person) is what makes the advice sound natural rather than rude or too weak.
